# Image assets & licensing

Every image in `public/images/` is a **real photograph** released under
**[CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)**,
sourced from [rawpixel's public-domain collection](https://www.rawpixel.com/category/53/public-domain)
via the [Openverse](https://openverse.org/) search API (filtered to CC0 / commercial-use licences).

- CC0 allows copying, modification and commercial use **without attribution**. Credits are listed below anyway.
- No images from the reference theme demo are used, and neither is the theme vendor's logo.
- No illustrations, drawings, paintings or cartoons. Several product shots are museum photographs of
  objects (LACMA, Art Institute of Chicago, Barnes Foundation, and others) released as CC0 open access.
- No branded or trademarked product packaging is shown.

Files are resized to at most 1024 px wide (the largest size rawpixel serves without an account) and
saved as optimised JPEGs. Product photos also had their light backdrop lifted to pure white (a per-channel
levels adjustment; for `group-10194.jpg`, a row-wise flat-field correction of its gradient backdrop), so
`mix-blend-mode: multiply` blends them seamlessly into the grey product cards. Under CC0 these edits are
permitted. The photos themselves are unaltered in content.

## Replacing images

Keep the same file path and name and no code changes are needed. Product image paths live in
`data/products.js`, and homepage banners in `data/home.js` and the section components.
